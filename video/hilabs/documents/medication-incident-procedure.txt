FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Riverside Hospital
Medication Incident Procedure, version 1.4

1. Report the incident in the incident system immediately.
2. Make the patient safe and inform the responsible physician.
3. The Chief Pharmacist reviews the report within 24 hours.
4. Serious incidents are escalated to the Medical Director within 48 hours.
5. A summary of every incident is reviewed at the monthly safety meeting.
