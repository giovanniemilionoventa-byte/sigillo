FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Riverside Hospital Pharmacy
Medication Dispensing Policy, version 3.2, effective 1 November 2026

1. Purpose
This policy sets out how the hospital pharmacy dispenses medication to wards and outpatients.

2. Verification
Every prescription for a controlled substance must be verified by two pharmacists before dispensing.

3. Records
Each dispensing event is logged with the time, the pharmacist and the prescription reference. Logs are kept for 10 years.

4. Errors and near misses
Any dispensing error or near miss is reported to the Chief Pharmacist within 24 hours.

5. AI assistants
An AI assistant may draft summaries and check prescriptions against this policy. It never dispenses and never overrides a pharmacist. Every action of the assistant is recorded.

Approved by: Chief Pharmacist (fictional)
