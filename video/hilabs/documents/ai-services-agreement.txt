FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Services Agreement

This agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Riverside Hospital (the "Hospital") and Northwind Health AI GmbH (the "Provider").

1. Services
The Provider supplies an AI assistant that summarises prescription records for the Hospital pharmacy.

2. Fees and payment
The Hospital pays EUR 3,900 per month. Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date.

3. Data protection
The Provider processes personal data only on the Hospital's written instructions and only inside the European Union.

4. Term
The agreement lasts 12 months and renews for 12 months unless either party gives 60 days' notice.

5. Governing law
German law applies. The courts of Berlin have jurisdiction.
